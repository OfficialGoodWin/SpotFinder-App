## Specific product judgement

SpotFinder is visibly designed around a real map: dense place symbols, outdoor category color, a map-first search, a distinctive green add control, and community spot sheets. The Journal uses a separate editorial serif hierarchy, which is appropriate to storytelling. These are more specific than a generic three-card SaaS template. The current weakness is that the map presents fallback “Featured community places” even when the current view contains no community places, and the editorial section has no story yet. [Map](screenshots/home-intro-desktop.png), [Featured](screenshots/featured-desktop.png), [Journal](screenshots/route-news.png).

Linear is stronger at publishing and scanning written updates. Komoot is stronger at showing what a selected geographic result offers before commitment. Mapy.com is stronger at giving a clear, detailed place information panel in this tested guest workflow. None of those observations implies SpotFinder needs their full route planning, enterprise workflow or content scale.

## Limits

No member-only reference workflows were claimed. AllTrails and Atlas Obscura access restrictions prevent substantive comparison. Synthetic browser speed was not normalized across sites, so this report does not rank their performance. Reference images are evidence of observed pages only; no reference assets or layouts were copied.
